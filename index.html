Bio-Inspired Event-Based Cameras: The Silicon Retina

How vertebrate retinal neurobiology revolutionized computational vision and dynamic sensors

  • ✦Traditional video cameras operate on an artificial clock (30–60 frames/sec), capturing static images regardless of motion.
  • ✦Biological eyes do not capture frames: 100+ million photoreceptors operate asynchronously, transmitting changes via action potentials.
  • ✦Neuromorphic Event-Based Cameras (Dynamic Vision Sensors - DVS) mimic the vertebrate retina at the hardware level.
  • ✦Each pixel has its own microscopic brain: it only transmits data when brightness changes.
Core Biological Principle
Only transmit information when something changes. Nature doesn't waste energy transmitting redundant static scenes.

Biological Insight
Human eyes perform continuous microscopic tremors (ocular micro-saccades) at ~50–100 Hz. Without this continuous motion, stationary objects would disappear from the visual field due to retinal adaptation!

3D Trajectory Settings

Why 3D Space-Time?
Standard video collapses time into discrete 33 ms bins. In event-based vision, time is a continuous coordinate. Algorithms fit spatio-temporal planes to extract optical flow and 3D depth directly from the event cloud!
